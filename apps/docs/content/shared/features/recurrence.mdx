<section id='behavior'>

## Native daily and weekly rules

The native feature accepts plain `rrule` objects with `freq: 'DAILY'` or `'WEEKLY'`, positive integer `interval`,
nonnegative integer `count`, and an inclusive Temporal `until`. Weekly rules also accept non-ordinal `byDay`
weekdays (`'MO'` through `'SU'`) and `wkst`, defaulting to Monday. An omitted `byDay` uses the seed weekday.
Daily rules do not accept `byDay` or `wkst`. Strings and unsupported selectors throw.

Weekly intervals are anchored to `wkst`, independently of the calendar's first weekday. Matching dates are sorted
and deduplicated; the seed is included only if it matches. COUNT applies to nominal starts before exclusions,
including starts before the visible range. COUNT and UNTIL may be combined.

`rdate`, `exdate` and independently bounded `exrule` objects or arrays use the same shape as the RRule package.
Exception rules use the same daily/weekly subset. Exclusions win over all inclusions and do not replenish COUNT.

<include lang='ts'>../../../examples/core/native-recurrence.ts#exceptions</include>

Both implementations share the time-zone, duration, store and occurrence behavior below. Native expansion seeks
directly into the range without replaying historical starts. Each rule query allows at most 10,000 daily or weekly
periods after seeking and clipping COUNT/UNTIL; exceeding the limit throws without returning partial results.

## Switching to RRule

Native definitions are structurally compatible with `createRRuleEvent`. Replace the factory and registered feature
to switch implementations. Matching occurrences keep their starts, ends, metadata and IDs. Both features may
coexist in one calendar.

## RRule strings and options objects

Add the optional `@calendar-tsx/rrule` package to expand repeating definitions in the visible range. It uses `rrule-temporal`
2.2.8 and keeps the core free of that dependency.

```bash
pnpm add @calendar-tsx/rrule
```

Register `rruleFeature` and create definitions with `createRRuleEvent`. Pass them in `events` alongside one-off
events. `start` owns the recurrence's seed and zone; `duration` supplies each occurrence's length.

<include lang='ts'>../../../examples/core/recurrence.ts#series</include>

Use an RRULE string or a plain options object. Both support monthly/yearly rules, ordinal weekdays and BYSETPOS.
Options use string frequencies and camel-case arrays such as `byDay: ['2FR']` and `bySetPos: [-1]`. Instantiated
rule objects are excluded; no constants import is needed.

<include lang='ts'>../../../examples/core/recurrence.ts#options</include>

Options use Temporal for `until` and omit `dtstart`/`tzid`. A string's embedded DTSTART must agree with `start`;
conflicting dates or zones throw. Strings can include DTSTART, RRULE, RDATE, EXDATE and EXRULE lines. They do not
accept complete VCALENDAR/VEVENT documents or RDATE periods.

`rdate` includes extra dates; `exdate` removes dates; `exrule` accepts one rule or an array of rules. Exclusions win
over all inclusions and do not replenish COUNT. A UTC UNTIL ending in `Z` is inclusive and compares actual instants.
Timed exception dates may use another zone. All-day exceptions and UNTIL use `Temporal.PlainDate`.

Each object-form `exrule` may set its own Temporal `start`; otherwise it inherits the series seed. COUNT and
INTERVAL run from that seed. UNTIL is inclusive. Timed seeds retain their own zone and clock time; all-day seeds
use `PlainDate`. Exclusion starts and bounds must match the parent event's date kind.

<include lang='ts'>../../../examples/core/recurrence.ts#bounded-exclusions</include>

This leaves January 5 and 12. Arrays can combine exclusions with different seeds and bounds. Exclusions remove
matching occurrence starts; they do not trim event durations. Timed matching compares instants across zones.
String exclusions and inline EXRULE lines inherit the parent seed.

## Time zones and duration

Timed occurrences keep the seed zone's wall-clock schedule. Changing the calendar's display zone converts the
results after expansion. Temporal's `compatible` rule shifts DST gaps forward and uses the earlier offset in folds;
an explicitly offset seed retains its instant. Gap candidates still count toward COUNT.

Timed duration must be positive, without years/months. Days and weeks are elapsed multiples of 24 hours. Start
nanoseconds are preserved. All-day duration uses whole days/weeks and defaults to one day; its generated end is
inclusive. Use daily or coarser frequencies for all-day rules.

## Occurrences and editing

Layouts expose concrete events with stable IDs and `occurrence: { seriesId, originalStart }`. `originalStart` keeps
the scheduled start before display-zone conversion. Resource IDs, background display and data pass through.
Expansion includes starts before the view when their duration overlaps it.

Event stores retain definitions. Update or remove the series by its ID. To replace one occurrence, exclude its
original start and add a one-off event. Replace definitions and arrays when editing; they are compared by reference.
For an initially empty store that will hold series, type its initial array as `Calendar_EventDefinition<EventData>[]`.

Clock ticks reuse expansion. Each definition caches at most four range/zone queries. In the RRule implementation,
the engine's full-result cache is disabled. Its nominal wall times travel as UTC Temporal values before the calendar
applies compatible DST resolution in the seed zone. Native expansion resolves nominal dates directly in that zone.

The RRule engine enforces default limits of 10,000 outer iterations and 1,000,000 candidate evaluations. Options objects
can set `maxIterations` and `maxCandidateEvaluations`. Exhaustion throws instead of returning partial occurrences;
large or complex queries can still be expensive. The former `rrule` subdaily selector stall no longer applies.
