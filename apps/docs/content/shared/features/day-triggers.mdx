<section id='intro'>

`DayGridViewDayTrigger`, `TimeGridViewColumnTrigger` and `TimelineViewDayTrigger` jump from a day to another view.
Pass `calendar={calendar}` and a registered `view` key. A click navigates through that instance's `onNavigate` on the trigger's date.

Use the typed instance returned by `useCalendar` or `createCalendar`, passing it down to your view parts. TypeScript
checks `view` against that instance's registered keys. An instance obtained from `useCalendarContext` has erased
view types and cannot provide that check.

- Your own `onClick` runs first. Call `event.preventDefault()` in it to skip the navigation.
- The default accessible name is "Go to" followed by the full date, in the provider's `locale`. Pass `aria-label` to
  replace it.
