<section id='intro'>

`DayGridViewDayTrigger`, `TimeGridViewColumnTrigger` and `TimelineViewDayTrigger` jump from a day to another view. Give them a `view` key, and a
click navigates to that view on the trigger's date, through `onNavigate`.

- Your own `onClick` runs first. Call `event.preventDefault()` in it to skip the navigation.
- The default accessible name is "Go to" followed by the full date, in the provider's `locale`. Pass `aria-label` to
  replace it.
