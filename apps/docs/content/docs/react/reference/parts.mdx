---

Every part renders one element with `data-scope='calendar'`, a `data-part` name, state attributes and its geometry as
`--cal-*` custom properties; [custom themes](/docs/react/styling/custom-themes#parts) lists them. Parts render your
children, accept any props of their element plus [`render`](rendering-parts), and forward `ref`.

All parts must render inside `CalendarProvider` or `Calendar`.
